Profile Image Instructions:
=======================

To add your profile picture:

1. Save your photo as "profile.jpg" in the public/assets/ folder
2. Make sure the image is:
   - Square aspect ratio (1:1) for best results
   - At least 400x400 pixels for high quality
   - JPG, PNG, or WebP format
   - Professional headshot or clear portrait

3. The image will automatically display when you add it
4. If no image is found, a beautiful gradient placeholder with your initial "N" will show instead

Current placeholder shows a gradient circle with "N" - this will be replaced when you add your photo!
